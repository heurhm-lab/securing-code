# Cardinal Logistics internal audit: ranked findings

*Synthetic data for training. Draft for review by counsel. Run real audits at counsel's direction to preserve privilege.*

Scope: 1,350 applicant and employee records (AEDT era 2024-07-01 to 2026-07-31, plus pre-AEDT incumbents) and the 18-month discipline record ending 2026-09-30.

**Bottom line.** 8 of 15 findings are likely violations, and 2 of those carry harm that is hard to undo (terminations tied to FMLA leave and concerted activity), so they come first. The AEDT's disparities trace to two affect-based subscores, so zero-weighting them is a proportionate first step that doesn't require scrapping the tool. The wage findings are fully curable with a payroll fix and back pay.

## How findings are ranked

Priority = likelihood × severity × reversibility weight (Low 1.5, Medium 1.2, High 1.0). Harm that's harder to undo moves up.

| Score | Likelihood | Severity |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | Violation shown on the face of the data | Systemic, many people, termination or willfulness exposure |
| 4 | Strong evidence: significant disparity plus direct evidence in records | Systemic or touching protected leave/disability/organizing; damages beyond back pay |
| 3 | Significant disparity or credible pattern; employer has a defense to build | Wage or classification exposure, recoverable with back pay and liquidated damages |
| 2 | Signal only: not significant, small sample, or facts missing from the data | Limited group or limited dollars |
| 1 | Verification item: no violation indicated, but the basis is undocumented | Minimal |

| Reversibility | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Low | Harm is hard to undo (lost jobs, terminations, chilled organizing): act first |
| Medium | Undoable with effort (re-evaluate applicants, rescind discipline, restore lost earnings) |
| High | Fully curable by paying money owed and fixing the rule |

## Remediation, in priority order

### 1. F08 · FMLA

**Legal basis:** FMLA 29 U.S.C. 2615(a)(1) interference; 29 C.F.R. 825.220(c)

**Proportionate step:** Now: rescind the points and any discipline built on them. Offer reinstatement and back pay to the terminated employees. Set the attendance system to exclude FMLA-designated time automatically. Retrain supervisors on how points work with protected leave.

### 2. F10 · NLRA

**Legal basis:** NLRA Sections 7 and 8(a)(1); non-union workplaces are covered

**Proportionate step:** Now: have counsel review each of these events. Rescind and expunge the discipline where the activity was protected, and offer reinstatement where it led to termination. Within 30 days: train supervisors on concerted activity. Any written posting or notice is a decision for counsel.

### 3. F01 · AEDT / ADA

**Legal basis:** ADA 42 U.S.C. 12112(b)(6)-(7): selection criteria that screen out people with disabilities; tests must measure skill, not impairment

**Proportionate step:** Now: route every accommodation request to a structured-interview alternative instead of AEDT scoring. Within 30 days: re-evaluate the 34 rejected requesters on the skills test and a structured interview, and offer the next opening to those who qualify. Require the vendor to document its accommodation process.

### 4. F07 · Premium OT algorithm / FMLA, ADA

**Legal basis:** FMLA 29 C.F.R. 825.220(c): leave can't be a negative factor in employment actions; ADA: restrictions can't reduce opportunities without an individualized assessment

**Proportionate step:** Now: remove FMLA-designated absences and accommodation restrictions from the reliability score. Within 30 days: estimate premium-overtime earnings each affected employee lost and pay it. Put any change to the algorithm's inputs through HR and legal sign-off.

### 5. F09 · ADA

**Legal basis:** ADA failure to accommodate and retaliation (42 U.S.C. 12112(b)(5)(A), 12203)

**Proportionate step:** Now: rescind these warnings. For each accommodated employee, set and document an adjusted productivity standard through the interactive process. Within 60 days: change the productivity reports so they flag employees with an active accommodation before any warning is issued.

### 6. F04 · FLSA

**Legal basis:** FLSA 29 U.S.C. 207(e): regular rate includes shift differentials; 2-year lookback (3 if willful) plus equal liquidated damages

**Proportionate step:** Now: fix the payroll rule at both sites. Within 30 days: recompute overtime for all affected employees, current and former, back 3 years, and pay the difference. Check whether other premiums (attendance bonus, lead pay) have the same problem.

### 7. F05 · FLSA

**Legal basis:** 29 C.F.R. 541.600: salary-level test; failing it defeats the exemption regardless of duties

**Proportionate step:** Now: either raise these salaries above the floor or reclassify them as non-exempt and start tracking hours. Pay back overtime for the lookback period. Add a salary-floor check to the compensation system so this can't recur.

### 8. F11 · NLRA

**Legal basis:** NLRA Section 8(a)(1); long-standing Board law on pay-discussion rules

**Proportionate step:** Now: rescind the rule and tell employees in writing that they may discuss pay. Remove discipline issued under it (covered in F10). Review the handbook for similar rules.

### 9. F02 · AEDT / ADEA

**Legal basis:** ADEA disparate impact (Smith v. City of Jackson): defense is a reasonable factor other than age; the four-fifths rule is a Title VII screening guide, not the ADEA test

**Proportionate step:** Now: set the engagement and communication subscores to zero weight in hiring decisions until validated. Within 90 days: get the vendor's validation study, or commission one, showing those subscores predict job performance. Keep the problem-solving subscore and the skills test, which show no age gap.

### 10. F03 · AEDT / Title VII

**Legal basis:** Title VII disparate impact (race, national origin); Connecticut v. Teal: a good bottom line does not excuse a discriminatory component

**Proportionate step:** Same subscore change as F02 (one fix covers both). For English-language communication, require a documented job analysis showing the level needed for warehouse roles. Re-run this analysis after 6 months of new applicants before drawing conclusions about the Hispanic ratio.

### 11. F12 · ADA / state law

**Legal basis:** ADA (leave as reasonable accommodation); state leave and disability laws in TN, MS, AR

**Proportionate step:** Within 60 days: HR reviews each case for an ADA request that should have opened the interactive process. Rescind points where it should have. Update the leave intake so a medical leave request that doesn't qualify for FMLA prompts an ADA review.

### 12. F06 · FLSA

**Legal basis:** 29 C.F.R. 541.200-202: administrative exemption needs discretion and independent judgment on significant matters

**Proportionate step:** Within 60 days: have counsel run a duties interview with each dispatcher and their manager. Reclassify any who don't meet the test, with back pay. No change before that review.

### 13. F13 · AEDT / monitoring

**Legal basis:** UGESP 29 C.F.R. 1607.4(D): small numbers need cautious interpretation; pool data over time

**Proportionate step:** Pool these groups' data over a rolling 24-month window and re-test at each quarterly review. Don't publish their ratios until each group has at least 30 applicants.

### 14. F14 · FLSA / verification

**Legal basis:** FLSA 29 U.S.C. 213(b)(1); SAFETEA-LU small-vehicle exception

**Proportionate step:** Within 60 days: document vehicle weights and interstate routes for each driver. Confirm whether state law follows the federal exemption.

### 15. F15 · NLRA / verification

**Legal basis:** NLRA Section 2(11) supervisor definition; Oakwood Healthcare test

**Proportionate step:** Confirm whether the person actually has authority to hire, fire, discipline or direct work using independent judgment. If not, review the event under F10.

## What this audit does not show

- **Passing the four-fifths rule is not proof of compliance.** Groups that passed still have subscore gaps (F02, F03). The Uniform Guidelines require a validation study for any procedure with adverse impact, and a disparity can exist without failing the ratio.
- **Small groups are not cleared.** A ratio isn't interpreted when a group has fewer than 30 applicants or fewer than 5 expected hires. Those groups are neither a finding nor evidence of fairness (F13).
- **Timing is evidence, not a conclusion.** Discipline soon after leave, an accommodation or concerted activity is a reason to review each file. It doesn't prove retaliation.
- **Narrative screening is rule-based.** Every Section 7 tier needs a person to read the full file before acting.
